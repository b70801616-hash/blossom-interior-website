Common Questions

Before you get in touch

How long does a full home interior take?

Most 2–3 BHK turnkey projects take 45–75 days from design approval to handover, depending on scope. We share a fixed timeline before work starts.

Do you handle both design and execution?

Yes — Blossom manages the full project in-house, from space planning and material selection through carpentry, painting, civil work and final styling.

Which areas do you serve?

We work across Thane, Kalyan, Dombivali and nearby areas, for both residential homes and commercial spaces such as offices, shops and salons.

Is the quote fixed, or can costs change later?

We share an itemised quote before starting. Any change you request mid-project is priced separately and approved by you before we proceed — no surprise billing.

Do you offer a warranty after handover?

Yes, every turnkey project includes a workmanship warranty, and our team remains reachable for support after the keys are handed back to you.
